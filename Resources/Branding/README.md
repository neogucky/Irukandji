# Alphane artwork

`Alphane-wordmark.png` is the artwork supplied by the project owner. `Alphane-symbol.png` is its isolated geometric A, produced using the built-in imagegen tool. The mark is black with transparent negative space; in-app assets render it as a template. Platform icon exports size and place the mark in the existing Xcode asset catalogs, preserving Apple TV foreground/background layers.

Extraction prompt:

> Extract only the existing geometric capital A above ALPHANE. Remove the wordmark and paper background, preserve the asymmetric silhouette, black strokes, folded ribbon geometry, and negative spaces. Produce a crisp, flat black symbol on genuine transparency, centered on a square canvas with even padding. No text, shadows, redesign, or new elements.

`Alphane-stone.png` is the light grey stone texture generated with the built-in imagegen tool for the Apple TV icon. The default Apple TV icon, its App Store export, and the matching alternate icon use this texture in the background layer. The existing transparent foreground symbol is unchanged, preserving parallax. `Alphane-tv-icon.png` previews the combined layers.

Stone texture prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: background texture layer for an Apple TV app icon. Create a flat, front-facing close-up of light grey honed natural stone, with very fine mineral grain and gentle low-contrast mottling. Neutral cool light grey, matte surface, even diffuse illumination across the entire image. Fill the entire canvas with stone texture. Restrained, modern, elegant, subtle enough to sit behind a black geometric logo. No logo, lettering, objects, borders, deep cracks, strong veins, dramatic shadows, bevels, gradients or vignette. Landscape canvas.
